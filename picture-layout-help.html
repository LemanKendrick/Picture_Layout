Picture Layout Planner – Help

Plan a wall of framed pictures, then get exact hanging measurements. Desktop/laptop only. Press F1 in the app to open this page.

Quick start

  1. On Set up your space, enter the wall width and height in inches and click Create space.
  2. In Frame Library (left panel), enter a frame and click Add to library.
  3. Drag a frame from Your Frames onto the space.
  4. Arrange — snapping, spacing labels and the nudge pad help you fine-tune.
  5. Optionally drop a photo onto a frame to preview the art.
  6. Click Export PDF for a printed hanging sheet or Export data (CSV) for a spreadsheet.
All dimensions are in inches. On-screen measurements round to the nearest 1/8"; the exports give decimal inches to 0.001".

The space (wall)

  • Space size – shown in the header. Change size lets you enter a new width and height. Frames already placed stay put, but undo history is cleared.
  • Canvas background – color picker or hex value (e.g. #E8E2D6) to approximate your wall paint.
  • Centerline – a gray dashed line runs top to bottom at the center of the space, handy for centering over a sofa or mantel.
  • The canvas scales to fit your window; measurements are always true inches.
Measure the space from a fixed reference you can find again with a tape — a corner, door casing, ceiling or chair rail. All hanging numbers are measured from the top-left corner of the space.

Frame library

Frames you add are saved in the browser and are there next time you open the app.

Each entry in Your Frames shows a small preview, size, molding and mat. Click × on an entry to remove it from the library (frames already on the canvas are not affected).

Editing a frame

  1. Click the pencil ✎ on a library entry. Its settings load into the form, which is outlined in blue and shows how many placed copies will change.
  2. Change any field and click Save changes, or Cancel edit to back out.

Every copy of that frame on the canvas updates: name, size, molding, colors and mat. Each copy keeps its top-left corner, picture and lock. If a larger size would push a frame past the edge of the space, it slides back inside.

Undo reverts the placed copies, but the library entry keeps the new settings. Edit it again to change it back.

Placing & moving frames

  • Drag a frame from Your Frames onto the space. It lands centered on the mouse. The same frame can be placed many times.
  • Each placed frame gets a number badge (top center), in the order placed. The PDF and CSV use the same numbers. Removing a frame renumbers the ones after it.
  • Click a frame to select it (blue outline); drag to move it. Frames can't be dragged off the space.
  • Click an empty part of the space to deselect.

Nudge pad & Turbo

The nudge pad appears at the lower right whenever a frame is selected.

  • Each arrow click moves the selected frame 1/8".
  • Check Turbo above the pad for 1/2" per click. The center of the pad shows the current step.
  • Arrows are grayed out when the selected frame is locked.

Alignment snapping

While dragging, a frame snaps when one of its edges or its center comes close to an edge or center of any other frame. A purple guide line shows what it lined up with. Snapping only applies while dragging — the nudge pad moves freely.

  • Solid line – edge to edge, or center to center.
  • Dashed line with diamond markers – the top or bottom edge of the frame you're moving lines up with the vertical midpoint of another frame. The diamonds mark that frame's midpoint on its left and right sides.

Measurements & guides

Show spacing

Orange dashed lines between neighboring frames (side by side or stacked), labeled with the gap. Use it to make spacing even.

Top left corner

For the selected frame, blue lines show the distance from the top of the space down to the frame's top edge, and from the left side of the space to the frame's left edge.

Pictures on frames

Drag an image file (JPG, PNG, etc.) from File Explorer onto a placed frame. It fills the opening inside the molding and mat. Images are included in the PDF diagram and saved with the layout.

  • Replace – drop a different image on the frame.
  • Remove – hover over or select the frame and click × picture at its bottom-right corner. The frame goes back to showing its name and size. Works on locked frames and can be undone.
Photos straight off a phone are large. Several of them can exceed the browser's storage, and the auto-saved layout may stop updating. Resize photos to around 1000 px first, or use Save layout to keep a file copy.

Locking & removing

  • Lock – click the padlock at the top-left of a frame. A locked frame shows a dashed gray outline and can't be dragged or nudged. Click the padlock again to unlock.
  • Remove – click the × at the top-right of a frame, or select it and press Delete.
  • Clear canvas removes all placed frames (asks first; can be undone).

The last 50 changes to placed frames (move, nudge, add, remove, lock, picture added or removed, clear, library edit) can be undone. Changes to the library itself and background color are not part of undo. Shortcuts are ignored while typing in an input box.

Saving layouts

  • Auto-save – the current layout (space, frames, background) is kept in the browser and reloads when you reopen the app.
  • Save layout – downloads picture-layout.json. Rename it per wall (e.g. hallway.json) to keep several plans.
  • Load layout – opens a saved .json and replaces the current layout.

Export (PDF & CSV)

Export PDF

Saves picture-layout-hanging-reference.pdf (letter size, orientation matches the space): page 1 is the scaled layout diagram with numbered frames, page 2 is the hanging data table. Works offline using the bundled libs folder.

Export data (CSV)

Saves hanging-data.csv for Excel or Google Sheets. One row per picture:

ColumnMeaning
Space Width / Height (in)Size of the space
Picture #Number badge
Frame Width / Height (in)Overall frame size
Top-Left X / Y (in)Frame's top-left corner, from the top-left of the space
Top-Center X / Y (in)Middle of the frame's top edge, from the top-left of the space

Using the hanging data on the wall

  1. Find the top-left corner of the space on the real wall (your reference).
  2. For each picture, measure across Top-Center X and down Top-Center Y; make a light pencil mark. That's where the top of the frame goes.
  3. On the back of the frame, measure from the top edge down to the hanger (wire pulled taut toward the top, sawtooth, or D-ring).
  4. Set the nail or hook that distance below the pencil mark.
Two D-rings: measure each ring's offset from the frame's center line and from the top, then mark both, left and right of the top-center mark. In the CSV, add a column for hanger drop and let the spreadsheet compute nail height.

Files & installation

C:\Custom_Apps\
  index.html
  picture-layout-help.html
  libs\
    jspdf.umd.min.js
    jspdf.plugin.autotable.min.js
  • Keep picture-layout-help.html and the libs folder in the same folder as the app — both are found by relative path.
  • Open the app from C:\Custom_Apps\, not from Downloads.
  • GitHub Pages: commit the help file alongside the app. Because the app is named index.html, the bare folder URL (e.g. https://<user>.github.io/<repo>/) opens it.
